# TimeboardApp Code of Conduct

## Purpose and scope

TimeboardApp is a place to discuss, build, document, and maintain useful software. Everyone participating in its issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, or other project-managed spaces must treat others with dignity. These standards also apply when someone represents the project elsewhere.

People are welcome regardless of age, disability, appearance, ethnicity, nationality, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity or expression, education, economic circumstances, or level of technical experience. Participation does not require agreement on every technical decision.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss the work rather than attacking the person. Explain concerns with evidence, examples, and proposed improvements.
- Respect accessibility needs, language differences, personal boundaries, and requests to stop unwanted contact.
- Give credit, acknowledge mistakes, and make corrections. Ask questions without belittling someone for not knowing an answer.
- Keep discussion relevant. Allow maintainers and contributors reasonable time to respond; participation is not an on-call support obligation.
- Protect private information. Use synthetic examples and redact credentials, task data, and identifying details before sharing evidence.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, stalking, discriminatory insults, sexualized attention, deliberate humiliation, impersonation, and sustained personal attacks are not permitted. Neither are publishing someone else's private information without permission, repeatedly contacting someone who has asked you to stop, coordinated disruption, spam, or retaliation against a person who raises a concern in good faith.

Disagreement, a declined feature request, or a critical technical review is not by itself misconduct. Claims of misconduct must not be used to suppress respectful criticism.

## Reporting

The repository owner, [@paulkakell](https://github.com/paulkakell), is the project moderation contact. For a project-specific concern, use a private contact method the owner has published. When GitHub offers **Report to repository admins** for the content, that is another route to the project maintainers. Do not assume this option or a private messaging service is enabled.

If no private project channel is available, ask the owner to establish one without naming participants or describing the incident publicly. Do not publish a conduct report in an issue or pull request. For behavior covered by GitHub's policies, including concerns involving a maintainer, use [GitHub's abuse-reporting process](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam). GitHub handles its own platform-policy decisions; that process is not a promise of project moderation or a substitute for emergency services.

Once a private channel is established, provide the relevant links, approximate dates, a factual description, and any immediate safety concern. Share only information needed to review the incident. Security vulnerabilities belong under [the security policy](SECURITY.md), not public bug forms or conduct reports.

## Review and enforcement

Maintainers should review reports fairly, limit disclosure to people who need the information, and explain any restrictions to the affected person when safe to do so. Complete confidentiality cannot be guaranteed, particularly when platform safety teams need to be involved. A maintainer directly involved in a complaint should not be its sole reviewer; use an uninvolved maintainer when available, or the platform-reporting route for platform-policy violations.

Depending on severity, pattern, and risk, maintainers may request a correction, give a warning, remove content, close or lock a discussion, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently exclude a participant. Serious threats or repeated harassment may justify immediate restrictions. Good-faith reports and cooperation with review must not result in retaliation.

A person subject to a moderation action may request reconsideration through the same private channel, explaining relevant new facts or a procedural concern. Respect the restriction while that request is considered. This policy does not guarantee response deadlines, access to confidential reports, or acceptance of a contribution.
